Before you ask

THE FOUR THINGS YOU'RE WONDERING

"Is my data private?"

Answer plainly, and say what you do not collect.

"How long does setup take?"

Give a number. Then explain what makes it that fast.

"Do I have to pay?"

State exactly what's free and where the line is.

"What if I already use X?"

Name the competitor. Explain the coexistence, not a takedown.
